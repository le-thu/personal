For now

A couple of weeks ago I bought the Lofree Flow 2 in black with Pulse tactile switches. Which is funny because when I wrote about my keyboard journey last year, I told myself I wouldn’t go back to low profile, that I was a linear switch person, and I really thought the Keychron Q1 Max was endgame.

Three keyboards later, here we are.

This keeps happening. I told myself I’d wait until at least the iPhone 19 when I got the 15 in Natural Titanium, certain that I didn’t want another black phone. Then the 17 came out in black and I switched back. The iPhone Air, which looked like an abomination to me when it launched, suddenly feels like a fine piece of hardware now that I’ve held it in person. I used to think screens didn’t belong in the bedroom, now we have a Samsung Frame and I love it. I used to think every desk needs the same keyboard for consistency. Each one of those felt completely settled when I said it. But none of them were. I wrote about some of these shifts before, but looking back, that was a list of arrivals. Now I’m noticing the pattern.

The phrase that keeps showing up in my journal is “I told myself I’d never.” The version of me writing that line really does believe it’s done. It’s only later, holding the next thing, that I notice the previous declaration was a snapshot, not a fact. I keep speaking like I’ve figured myself out and then very quickly disproving it. I’m not actually more consistent at 30 than I was at 25, I just declare things with more confidence.

It’s also funny how convincing each version of me sounds. I genuinely believed I was done with low profile. I would have argued for it. I believed nothing beats living in the city center because I get to be in the middle of everything. Now I love living in a quieter, more local neighborhood with actual scenery from my windows. Same with the office. I used to think I needed to be there to do real work, and now some of my best days are working from the home studio.

Imagine other things I’m completely sure about — for now — that I haven’t gotten around to doubting yet. One day, some of them are going to flip 180 and I won’t see it coming. To be honest, that actually sounds exciting now.

Six months from now I’ll probably disagree with something in this post. I’m looking at it as a good thing. Maybe the point.
